## About This Game

**The year is 878. The Great Heathen Army holds the Danelaw, Alfred holds
Wessex, and every quarrel in Britain is settled the same way: at the moot,
with steel.**

BRETWALDA: BLOOD MOOT is a fast directional-melee brawler. Read your foe's
swing, block on the true side, dodge what cannot be blocked, and answer
with your own edge. Five warrior classes — Huscarl, Berserker, Warden,
Runekeeper, Burhweard — each with real weight, real reach and one power
worth timing.

**Fight the modes the period actually had.** The Blood Moot free-for-all.
The tournament bracket, crowned round by round. The Burh — hold the walls
with your shield-brothers against waves that do not tire. War seasons where
your kingdom's every win moves the map, and one fighter is crowned
Bretwalda when the season ends.

**Sworn to a people, dressed by the finds.** Swear to the Anglo-Saxons, the
Norse, the Britons or the Picts and wear their true colours — dye vats
tuned against the archaeology, not a palette picker. Every device in the
game is sourced to a real object or labelled an invention in as many
words: the Sutton Hoo helmet, the seax, the raven banner taken at Cynwit,
woad war paint the way Caesar described it, pattern-welded and
serpent-marked blades out of Beowulf's own vocabulary.

**Bring your warband.** Party up, queue together, fight together. Your
profile, your marks and your hoard walk between browser and desktop —
same account, either door.
